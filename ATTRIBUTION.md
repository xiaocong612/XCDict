# Attribution & Third-Party Credits

XCDict utilizes curated open-source linguistic datasets to provide developers and learners with a high-performance, royalty-free, multi-tier English-Chinese dictionary.

---

## 1. Primary Vocabulary & Lexical Datasets

### KyleBing / english-vocabulary
- **Source**: [https://github.com/KyleBing/english-vocabulary](https://github.com/KyleBing/english-vocabulary)
- **License**: BSD 3-Clause License

- **Credit**: Thanks to KyleBing and open-source contributors for compiling and formatting the original lexical resources.

## 3. Original & Independent Contributions

- **Chinese Culture & Global Loanwords Corpus**:
  Over 120 curated cultural concepts (including *jiaozi, dim sum, wonton, tofu, bok choy, chow mein, congee, kung fu, tai chi, feng shui, yin yang, boba, tanghulu, anime, safari*, etc.) with bespoke modern Chinese definitions, etymological culture notes, and bilingual examples.
- **Data Cleansing & Index Optimization**:
  Automated data cleansing removing invalid glyphs, legacy syntax errors, indexing optimization for SQLite (instant sub-millisecond B-Tree queries), and standardized IPA pronunciation enrichments.
